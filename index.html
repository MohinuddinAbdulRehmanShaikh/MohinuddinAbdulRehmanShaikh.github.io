Two halves of the same job

I'm from Mumbai, where I studied Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at KJ Somaiya Institute of Technology. Alongside classes I ran the student council, which is where I first had to get a large group moving in one direction.

After graduating I worked as an analyst at three companies, ending at InCred Money, where I built the data foundation for a newly acquired trading product. I started at Purdue in Fall 2026.

Building with data

I like building the thing that makes a recurring question go away. At InCred Money, a trading product came in through an acquisition with no data pipeline behind it, while marketing was already ramping up. In the first sprint I proposed a temporary database that needed one manual update a day, so the team could start. Then I built the AWS pipeline that replaced it.

Working with people on decisions

Asked to work on pricing for unlisted shares, I started by understanding how information flowed through the business, then met the CEO to understand the end goal. My proposal caused friction with the research team at first. We got past it by working on their actual bottlenecks: there was no inventory system to track shares coming in and going out, and too few providers to get competitive prices.
